BEHIND THE CHAIR

Built from the dorms up.

Rayvon started cutting hair in the 10th grade — first teammates and younger brothers, then classmates and neighbors. Most of the craft was self-taught, with an early foundation shaped by his friend Marqeese.

At Texas Tech, dorm cuts became student-apartment cuts, then an independent barber studio. The Green Room is the next chapter: one room, one chair, and an experience built around authenticity.

"We all have a room. Mine happens to be green."
